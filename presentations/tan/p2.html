Share issuance and cross-sectional returns
Jeffrey Pontiff and Artemiza Woodgate
Journal of Finance 63 (2008), 921–945

Abstract

Pontiff and Woodgate document that share issuance predicts the cross-section of returns in the post-1970 period: firms that issue shares subsequently underperform, and firms that repurchase outperform. The issuance effect is distinct from the size, book-to-market, and momentum effects, and remains after controlling for them.
